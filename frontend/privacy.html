PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

What the tracker collects, why it is used, where third-party services are involved, and how you can control local data.

What we collect

Noida Bus Tracker does not require an account and does not intentionally ask for your name, phone number, email address, or home address.

Location

When you choose Use my location, your browser provides latitude and longitude to the website so the backend can find nearby buses. Location access is initiated by you through the browser's permission prompt.

Searches

When you search for a place, the search text is sent to our backend to request geocoding results.

Local browser data

The site may store theme preference, dismissed notices, favourites, nearby-bus history and nearby-alert preferences in your browser's local storage.

Why we use it

We use location and search information only to provide the nearby-bus and map features you request. Local storage is used to keep product preferences and support features such as movement history, favourites and dismissed notices.

Bus reports

If you submit a report, we receive the bus identifier, report category, any optional note you enter, and the consent value required by the form. Do not include personal information in the note.

Reports are submitted through Netlify Forms. Netlify documents that form submissions are stored in its user database and may include submission metadata, and recommends active management and deletion when forms contain personal information.

Third parties

Maps and geocoding use MapTiler services and OpenStreetMap data. The website is hosted on Netlify, while the bus API is hosted on Render. Their own terms and privacy practices may apply to the services they operate.

Analytics

We do not add client-side analytics scripts to this site. Netlify Web Analytics, when enabled for the site, is based on CDN/server-side data rather than a client tracking script or analytics cookie.

Your controls

You can deny browser location permission, stop using the location feature, clear site data in your browser, or reset the local data stored by this site.
