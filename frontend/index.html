DATA & TRUST

Useful, with the caveats visible.

Bus positions come from MARGDARSHI · UPSRTC. “Moving towards” and the likely last stop are estimates based on recent GPS movement and mapped stop coordinates, not confirmed dispatch information.

Some buses may not appear if they are missing from the source feed, have no recent GPS signal, or do not have enough recorded history. We cannot confirm the location of buses that the source does not provide.
