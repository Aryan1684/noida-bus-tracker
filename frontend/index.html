UP TRADE FAIR · 25–29 SEP 2026

Free travel on 25 selected buses

During the UP Trade Fair, free bus travel is being provided on 25 selected buses from 25 September to 29 September.

How to identify them: the selected buses have two “निःशुल्क सेवा” (Nisul Seva) stickers, one on the front and one on the rear of the bus.

The live GPS feed does not identify which buses are part of the 25-bus free-service group, so the stickers on the bus are the reliable way to confirm.
