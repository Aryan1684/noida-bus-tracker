Cookies & Local Storage

A practical explanation of browser storage and third-party services used by the tracker.

Cookies

Noida Bus Tracker currently does not intentionally set a first-party cookie for its core tracker features. Our own preferences are primarily stored using localStorage.

What local storage may contain

  • Theme preference
  • Dismissed notices
  • Favourite locations
  • Nearby-bus movement history
  • Nearby-alert preference and last alert time
  • Acknowledgement of the movement warning

Third-party storage and services

Embedded or linked services can operate under their own policies. MapTiler requires visible attribution for its maps and OpenStreetMap data.

Netlify Forms stores form submissions in its user database.

We do not use a third-party advertising network on the tracker.

Consent approach

Because the tracker does not currently deploy optional client-side advertising or analytics cookies, there is no separate “accept all cookies” banner. If we introduce optional tracking technologies later, this notice will be updated and the consent model will be revisited.

How to clear storage

Use your browser's site-data controls, or use the reset button on the Privacy page. Clearing local storage can remove favourites, local history and preferences stored by the tracker.
