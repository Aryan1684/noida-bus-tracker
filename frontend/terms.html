Rules and limitations for using this independent bus-tracking service.

Service

Noida Bus Tracker is an independent web project that presents bus-location information and related tools. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with UPSRTC, Noida Authority, or any government department.

Live data

Bus positions come from MARGDARSHI / UPSRTC. The availability, freshness and accuracy of upstream data are outside our control. A position may be delayed, unavailable, stale or inaccurate.

Movement and routes

Where shown, “Moving towards” is an estimate based on available GPS movement and route context. It is not a guaranteed destination, route, arrival time or service commitment.

Because there are no paid transactions, a refund or cancellation policy is currently not applicable. This section will be updated if paid services are introduced.
